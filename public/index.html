HOW THE WEBSITE WORKS

Recover. Match. Reuse.

Urban Material Bank connects reusable construction materials with the people who need them. The platform turns leftover building inventory into searchable, verifiable and locally reusable resources.

01

Recover

Before demolition or renovation, identify potentially reusable bricks, blocks, doors, windows, steel and tiles.

Before demolition
02

List

Seller creates a listing with category, quantity, price, location, quality, condition, age, previous use, dimensions and actual photographs.

Structured inventory
03

Verify

Accounts are authenticated, while Urban Material Bank reviewed inventory can receive a separate verified listing status. New listings start unverified.

Trust layer
04

Match

Buyers search and filter by category, location, quality and price. Smart matching checks category, quantity and proximity to rank suitable inventory.

Matching algorithm
05

Inspect

Buyers can open the Material Passport and request a live or on-site inspection to check visible condition, quantity and authenticity before purchase.

Buyer confidence
06

Reuse

Once the material is sold, the seller can mark the listing as sold. Reusable inventory stays in circulation instead of becoming demolition waste.

Circular outcome
BEHIND THE MATCH

How our smart matching works

For the prototype, candidate listings are first filtered for the required material category and sufficient available quantity. Suitable listings are then ranked using location and match quality so buyers see the most practical options first.

Requirement→Category→Quantity→Distance→Best matches
🌱 Environmental layerMatched materials can show prototype estimated avoided CO₂ using category-based reuse factors.
📍 Location layerApproximate Bengaluru inventory locations help buyers discover supply closer to their project.
🔐 Security layerAuthenticated ownership controls protect seller listings and prevent unauthorized editing.
