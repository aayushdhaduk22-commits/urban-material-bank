How demo delivery is calculated

Delivery and the grand total appear only after a valid destination Maps link and the driving distances from Google Maps are supplied. Each pickup is calculated separately. Distances are user-entered, not automatically retrieved.

Per pickup: trips × (₹650 base + entered driving km × ₹23/km + ₹700 loading/unloading if selected). Demo capacity: 750 kg per trip. Weight assumptions: bricks 3 kg/piece, blocks 12, doors 30, windows 20, tiles 2.5 kg per listed unit; quantities listed in kg use their actual quantity.

These are illustrative rates, not a third-party quote. Actual routes, weights, dimensions, availability, taxes, tolls and final charges require carrier confirmation. Material quality must be inspected before purchase.
